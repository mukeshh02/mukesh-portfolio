BEHIND THE LENS

HI, I'M MUKESH.

A Delhi-based candid wedding photographer and storyteller with an obsession for raw, cinematic light and genuine human connection.

Photography for me isn't about stiff poses or artificial smiles. It is about capturing that quiet glance across a crowded banquet hall, the tear your father wipes away during the vidaai, and the ecstatic joy of dancing until 4 AM.

Over the last decade, I’ve had the privilege of documenting stories across India, Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Every wedding is a distinct movie, and I am honored to frame yours.
